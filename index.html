How to play

Your photo is the level.

  1. 1
    Snap, drop or paste a photo

    Edges become platforms, dark corners grow spikes, bright highlights turn into stars.

  2. 2
    Run to the green flag

    ←→ or AD to run · Space to jump (hold = higher) · R restart. Thin glowing lines are jump-through.

  3. 3
    Beat the clock, share the link

    Every level is proven beatable by a solver. Send it to a friend and race your time.

Everything runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
